Projects

Rennlog

Rennlog is the best way for car enthusiasts to learn about vehicle history and track maintenance for cars. It creates a durable record by VIN and acts as a source of truth for owners. You can simply send your records and they are associated with your vehicle — Rennlog handles the rest. When you go to sell your vehicle, we create a packet which can be transmitted with the car.

Crossle

Crossle is a daily word game. Drag twelve letters onto an empty grid and arrange them into a single connected crossword, using every letter, with no word shorter than three. Words are checked against the Collins Scrabble dictionary. New puzzle every day, and it keeps your streak.

Interview Query

Interview Query is an online adaptive learning platform focused on helping people get jobs in data professions. It has been likened to leetcode for data science.

Skada

Skada is a B2B SaaS tool focused on streamlining commercial insurance applications and workflows. in Swedish, skada refers to an insured event that has occurred and resulted in damage, loss, or injury.
